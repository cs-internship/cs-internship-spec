# CS Internship Program

## 📌 Purpose
We are a community of technical professionals who voluntarily teach and learn from each other **constructive impact skills** and **economic value creation**, in order to realize our vision and advance the mission of the teams, companies, organizations, and societies we belong to.

## 🌍 Vision
Transform the economic situation of millions worldwide by developing our capacity to creatively solve business problems using **technical, specialized, communicative, and interactive skills**.

## 🎯 Mission
1. Cultivate technical and specialized skills  
2. Develop communication, collaboration, teamwork, impact, value creation, and problem-solving capabilities  
3. Foster innate managerial and business abilities of members  

---

## 🏛️ CS Identity
**CS stands for Crystalline Society.**  
You can study more about the [**Declaration of CS Culture**](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/what-cs-culture-mehran-davoudi/), initiated by its original creator [**Prof. Ayaz Isazadeh**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ayazisazadeh/), to understand the philosophical and cultural foundation of this community.

---

## ⚖️ Core Principles
- **Value Creation:** Knowledge is transformed into real-world impact and economic value.  
- **Simulation of Modern Work Environments:** Exercises and processes mirror real organisational challenges.  
- **Self-Organization:** Members can propose and implement changes to rules and structures.  
- **Service & Commitment:** No money is exchanged; each participant provides service in return for service received.  
- **Resource Management:** Time and energy of volunteers are the most valuable resources.  
- **Motivation Management:** The only reward is employability — motivation must be intrinsic.  
- **Admission Process:** Orientation → Interview → Acceptance → Commitment declaration.

## 🏗️ Program Structure

### Tracks
- **C#**  
- **Web (JavaScript)**  
- **Machine Learning (Python)**  
- **Web (Blazor)** → *Will be available soon...*  
- **Cross Platform (MAUI Blazor)** → *Will be available soon...*  
- **Machine Learning (ML.NET)** → *Will be available soon...*  

### Stages
1. **Self-Learning (≈9 months)**  
2. **Internship (6 months)**  

### Parallel Pathways
- **Technical Skills:** Study Items, Tasks, technical sessions, peer-support.  
- **Business Skills:** Mentor Training, reports, workshops.  
- **Service:** From Step 5, interns join the mentoring team and practice organizational roles.  

---

## 📚 Self-Learning Stage
- ~10 steps, each with:
  - Study Items (technical materials)  
  - Tasks (practical exercises)  
  - Mentor Training (videos, reports, workshops)  

Interns also participate in peer-support sessions and technical mentoring sessions.  

---

## 👥 Mentoring Team Levels
1. **Intern Mentor** → Coordinator role, guiding new interns.  
2. **Assistant Mentor** → Technical mentor, leading sessions and tracking activities.  
3. **Candidate Mentor** → Observer in workshops, contributor in structural meetings.  
4. **Mentor** → Full mentor, leading workshops and shaping governance.  

---

## 📏 Rules & Processes
- **Commitment Management:** Interns declare deadlines for each step.  
- **Daily Reporting:** Submit daily progress reports.  
- **Social Media Activity:** Build personal brand via LinkedIn and X.  
- **Online Presentation:** 20-minute presentation at the end of each step.  

---

## 🚀 Outcome
Graduates of the CS Internship Program reach the level of **Junior Software Engineer**, equipped with technical, business, and managerial skills, ready to create real economic value in modern organizations.
