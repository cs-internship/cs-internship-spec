## What Was Adapted

The study items, tasks, acceptance criteria, and submission evidence of the bootcamp's 13 [course steps](https://github.com/CrystaCode/full-stack-ai-bootcamp/tree/2f4c6772d73200b92b54bfad65609e5df16b7368/course), and the outcome identifiers of its [coverage map](https://github.com/CrystaCode/full-stack-ai-bootcamp/blob/2f4c6772d73200b92b54bfad65609e5df16b7368/design/course-map.md). This course combines them with the [C# course](../csharp/README.md) of this repository into 14 steps.

[course-map.md](course-map.md) maps every bootcamp outcome and task to its step and lists every change: the new step order, split tasks, wording changes, and dropped sections.

This repository is the source of truth for the course in the CS Internship program.

## Reviewing Upstream Changes

The bootcamp is updated actively, and the mentor team reviews its changes periodically (proposed: every quarter, and before any major revision of this course):

1. Open the [comparison between the adapted commit and the current upstream branch](https://github.com/CrystaCode/full-stack-ai-bootcamp/compare/2f4c6772d73200b92b54bfad65609e5df16b7368...main).
2. For each changed upstream step, find the affected step and task in [course-map.md](course-map.md). In the step files, bootcamp tasks carry an invisible `<!-- bc: NN-Tm -->` marker.
3. Apply the changes that fit the program, keep the adaptations recorded in the course map, and update the course map.
4. Record the new upstream commit and its date under [Upstream Version](#upstream-version).
