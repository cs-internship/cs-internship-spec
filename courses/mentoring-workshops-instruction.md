# Mentors Training Workshop
## Introduction
- One of the main learning paths in the **CS Internship** program is the **Business Skills Learning Path**, also known as the **Soft Skills Learning Path**.
- The goal of the **Business Skills Learning Path** is to acquire a set of skills alongside technical expertise, which can transform any workforce into an impactful and influential presence in the business environment. By developing these soft skills, individuals can become **influential contributors within the business space**, complementing their technical abilities with *effective communication, problem-solving, leadership, and other valuable competencies*.
- **Mentorship workshops** are typically organized by professionals who have expertise in the workshop's subject area. These workshops are initially conducted with the participation of individuals who meet the eligibility criteria at the time of workshop formation.
- The development of these skills in the CS Internship program is achieved through participating in a series of workshops and exercising the provided training, each designed with a specific goal to acquire various soft skills. These workshops are **tailored to CS Internship participants** to help them develop and enhance their soft skills effectively.
- The sessions of the first series of workshops are already recorded and made available to individuals who later join this path. Since you are reading this page, it means you are going to watch the workshop videos.

## What prerequisites you should have?
In each step's file, there is a section called **"Mentors Training"**. This section contains links to at least one video. You should follow the instructions below and watch each of the videos as soon as you reach the corresponding step. 

- Before proceeding, ensure that you have access to the specified videos. If you don't have access, inform your coordinator. 
- Ask your coordinator to grant you access to the CS Internship **"Operational Board"** in **Microsoft Azure DevOps**.
- Set a session with your coordinator to make sure that you have access to this board as well as to the workshop videos. 
- You can access the report template through this [link](https://github.com/cs-internship/cs-internship-spec/blob/master/processes/Intern%20documents/CSI%20Templates/CSI%20-%20Mentor%20Training%20Report%20-%20%5BIntern's%20Name%5D%20-%20%5BCourse%20Name%5D%20-%20%5BVideo%20No%5D%20-%20Farsi.docx).
- Remember that mentorship workshop capacity in the program is limited, so, in order to meet your step's deadline, start this part of each step with higher priority, even though it is placed at the end of the step's document. 
  
## How to watch videos? 
- In the mentors training path, you watch the workshop videos provided in your step's file and apply their content accordingly. What you should do in this part can be abbreviated into four main sequential tasks: **Watch**, **Think**, **Write** and **Talk**
  1. **Watch** the specified video. Watch it attentively and with focus! Make sure you have prepared a proper environment and settled in a suitable situation to watch it. In this way, you can have the highest level of concentration.
  2. **Think** about the discussed concepts in the video in your own daily attitude and behavior. Your task is to observe and identify the concepts you heard in the video throughout your daily life. 
  3. **Write** a report about what you understood from the video. In this report, you should:
	  - write your understandings of the proposed concepts in the video.
	  - write your answers to the questions asked in the video.
	  - write at least three paragraphs about your relevant experiences and observations related to the concepts in the video.
  4. **Talk** about the observations you had between watching the video and attending the mentorship workshop.

## How to participate in the mentors training workshops?
### Default Instruction: 
1. After you have watched each video, you must write your report about it and send it to your coordinator.
2. You should set up a meeting for yourself. You can see how to set up a meeting through this [tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pRCOpFa5XlI). In the meeting, you have a chance to share your observations and understandings about the concepts proposed in the video.
3. You will attend each mentorship workshop with only **one** report. 
    - **Note**:
        - In mentorship workshop meetings, you need to turn on your camera, because the meetings will be held visually. 
	    - If you think that you are ready to watch the next video and attend a single mentoring workshop with two reports, then you can use the alternative instruction explained in the next part.
4. Since the mentorship workshop links in MS Teams are available via the Coordination-Meetings Telegram group, you must be present at the specified time and date for the session as scheduled.
5. You can watch the next video at least 7 days after the fist one, and repeat the default instruction for it.

### Alternative Instruction:
- If you want to pass the mentorship workshops faster, it is possible to participate with two reports in a single workshop. For this purpose, please follow the instructions below:
  
1. Watch the first mentorship workshop video in your step.
2. Send the report of the first video to your coordinator and set up a meeting for yourself.
3. If you are ready to start the next video, then ask your coordinator to forward your first report to a mentor to assess its quality.

5. The mentor will set a deadline to declare their assessment result about your report to your coordinator.
	- A **positive** assessment result, means then you can start to watch the second video of the step, write its report and send it to your coordinator before the already reserved mentorship workshop. 
	- A **negative** assessment result, means that you have to attend separate workshops for each mentorship video. This interval would help the concepts proposed in the videos to become clearer for you.

- **Attention!**
    - Please keep in mind that mentor availability to review your reports is not guaranteed.
    - Also, there is no guarantee that mentors will have time to check the report during the current sprint. So, it is almost 50-50 chance to get permission to participate in a workshop with two reports.
    - Furthermore, the deadline for reviewing your reports may be set in a way that does not accelerate the scheduling of mentorship sessions.
